# Anatomy content review register

## Scope

The first released landmark set covers the nine GLB specimens currently shipped by Corpus. Landmark coordinates are migrated from the anatomy project's authored model metadata and use the same normalized scene size. The short explanations in `components/anatomy/organ-data.ts` were reviewed against the sources below on 2026-08-06.

These landmarks are educational orientation aids. They are not surgical navigation data, diagnostic advice, or a substitute for a clinically reviewed atlas.

## Source register

- Heart chambers and mitral valve: [StatPearls: Anatomy, Thorax, Mitral Valve](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK549884/)
- Brain lobes and cerebellum: [StatPearls: Neuroanatomy, Cerebral Hemisphere](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK549789/) and [StatPearls: Neuroanatomy, Cerebellum](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK538167/)
- Lungs, trachea, and bronchi: [StatPearls: Anatomy, Airway](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK459258/) and [StatPearls: Anatomy, Thorax, Bronchial](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK537353/)
- Liver: [StatPearls: Physiology, Liver](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK535438/)
- Kidneys: [StatPearls: Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis: Kidneys](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK482385/)
- Eye: [StatPearls: Anatomy, Head and Neck, Eye](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK482428/) and [StatPearls: Anatomy, Head and Neck, Eye Cornea](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK470340/)
- Intestine: [StatPearls: Physiology, Small Bowel](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK532263/)
- Skin: [StatPearls: Anatomy, Skin (Integument)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK441980/)
- Pancreatic duct and its relationship to the duodenum: [StatPearls: Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis, Pancreas](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK532912/)
- Bile, portal vein, and duodenum relationships: [StatPearls: Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis: Duodenum](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK482390/)
- Portal-vein branching and surgical relevance: [StatPearls: Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis, Portal Venous System](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK554589/)
- Ureter and collecting-system relationships: [StatPearls: Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis Ureter](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK532980/)

## Release standard

Before additional landmarks are published, verify both conditions:

1. The structure is visible and unambiguous in the exact shipped model and view.
2. Its concise explanation is checked against an authoritative anatomy source and, for detailed clinical material, reviewed by a qualified anatomy educator or clinician.
